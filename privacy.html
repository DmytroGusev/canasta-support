Privacy policy · Canasta Salon

Effective 24 October 2026. Developer: Dmytro Husiev.

Data not collected by the app

Canasta Salon has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, server or third-party SDK. It does not collect or transmit personal data. Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. Standard iOS backups and Apple’s platform diagnostics are governed by your Apple settings and policies.

Opening a support link, GitHub issue or external rules page contacts that service under its own privacy policy. Information you choose to post to GitHub is public. We use support information only to respond and resolve the reported issue.

Delete the app and its data to remove local progress. For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the support repository. Do not post sensitive information.
